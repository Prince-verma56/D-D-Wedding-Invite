## Final prompts

Each final prompt combines this prefix, its subject below, and the suffix.

Prefix: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative photograph for the curved top-right image panel of a premium wedding event card. Generate a wide landscape photograph, 3:2 aspect ratio.

Lotus: A polished brass urli bowl with large vivid pink lotus flowers floating on dark water and a few rose petals, at an Indian wedding. Close detailed editorial photograph. Main lotus and bowl occupy the RIGHT TWO THIRDS of the image, with the upper half carrying the pink petals. Deep brown-wine shadows, restrained warm amber candlelight, natural floral texture, elegant and believable.

Lantern: An ornate glowing brass hanging lantern surrounded by fresh yellow and orange marigold garlands and dark green leaves at an Indian wedding. Close detailed editorial photograph. Main lantern occupies the RIGHT THIRD of the image, hanging from the top. Deep wine-brown shadows, warm golden light through perforated metal, realistic texture, elegant and believable.

Brocade: A close editorial still life of rich red Indian bridal silk fabric with intricate antique gold zardozi embroidery, folded gently under warm light. No people, no body, no jewellery labels. The most detailed ornate embroidery occupies the RIGHT TWO THIRDS of the image. Deep burgundy shadows, subtle gold glints, realistic fine textile texture, elegant and believable.

Suffix: Edge-to-edge photograph only; no typography, logos, watermark, borders, UI, or card mockup. Keep the subject in the upper/right area so a curved mask across the lower left can reveal it.
